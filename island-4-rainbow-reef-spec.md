# Island 4 — "Rainbow Reef" — Full Design Specification

**Sea:** 1 (Calm Sea, Pre-A1) · **Position:** second standard island · **Est. play time:** 35–50 min across 3–5 sessions
**Grammar focus:** *It is + color/adjective*; adjective-before-noun order; plural *-s*
**Prerequisites:** Island 3 (copula mechanic established; ≥80% of its vocab at "Known")
Shared framework (level cycle, feedback rules, SRS behavior, mode differences, ASR rules): identical to the Island 3 spec — this document details only what is island-specific.

## 2. Content Inventory

**New vocabulary (22):**
Colors (10): red, blue, green, yellow, orange, purple, pink, black, white, brown
Numbers (10): one, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, nine, ten
Counting nouns (2): shell, star
(*fish* is deliberately deferred — irregular plural would muddy the -s lesson; it arrives in Animal Island with an explicit "tricky word" star.)

**Chunks (6):**
1. What color is it?
2. It's red / It's a red shell.
3. How many stars?
4. Three! / Three stars!
5. Look!
6. One, two, three... (counting-up chant, taught as a song)

**Hebrew-transfer targets this island:**
- **Adjective order:** Hebrew puts the adjective after the noun (*dag kachol* = "fish blue") → the Paint Bucket mechanic (Level 5) makes English order physically necessary.
- **Plural -s:** Hebrew pluralizes with suffixes (-im/-ot) so the concept transfers, but the tiny /s/–/z/ ending is easily dropped in speech → the Twin Sticker mechanic makes it visible and audible.
- **/θ/ in "three":** Hebrew has no /θ/ → *three* vs *tree* is the game's first formal minimal-pair (Level 3B and Parrot Ears bonus rounds). CAPT feedback, if mic enabled: tongue-between-teeth animation on the parrot, mimicry-based, celebratory only.
- Easier-than-Hebrew note (use for confidence): English colors don't change with gender — one form fits everything. The parrot points this out once in Readers mode: "באנגלית זה קל — 'red' מתאים לכל דבר!"

## 3. Island Map Screen

An underwater-adjacent reef cove: level markers are giant shells along the shore; the mastery flag is a rainbow that gains color bands as SRS mastery grows (10 bands = 10 colors — the meter itself is disguised review; tapping a band says its color). Boss lair: the Rainbow Octopus's cave, fogged until Level 6.

## 4. Level-by-Level Design (island-specific)

### Level 1 — "The Painted Reef" (Listen & Tap)
First half — colors: gray reef scene; each intro paints part of it ("Blue!" → the water floods blue; "Yellow!" → the sun lights up). The world being colored *by* the words is the dual-coding hook. Practice: "Tap something red!" — multiple valid answers accepted (any red object), teaching category not single-object mapping.
Second half — numbers: shells wash ashore one at a time with the counting chant; practice: "Tap number seven!" on a shell-number line, then "Count the stars!" (tap each star, the game counts aloud with the child's taps).

### Level 2 — "Reef Pairs" (Picture Match)
Round 1: color word audio ↔ paint-splash swatch. Round 2 (retrieval): number audio ↔ *quantity groups* (audio "four" → tap the card showing 4 shells — quantity, not numeral, so pre-readers aren't blocked and the concept stays meaning-first). Readers round 3: written color words ↔ swatches (decodable: red, black, pink, ten, six; camera words: blue, one, two, eight).

### Level 3A (Readers) — "Message in a Bottle II" (Word Builder)
Spell: red, ten, six, big-hint bonus *sun* (review), plus camera-word shape-matching for *blue* and *two* (silent letters flagged with the star marker).

### Level 3B (Pre-Readers) — "Parrot Ears: Three or Tree?" (Sound Match)
(a) The flagship /θ/ minimal pair: parrot says "three" or "tree", child taps the 3-shells card or the palm-tree card — 8 trials, generous, errors just replay both words slowly with the tongue animation. (b) First-sound game with /r/–/b/–/g/ color words. (c) Counting claps 1–5.

### Level 4 — "Reef Orders" (Listen & Do, TPR)
Commands combine the island's two systems: "Tap three red shells!", "Put two stars in the box!" (previewing Toy Island's prepositions), "Jump four times!", "Paint the crab green!" (drag paint bucket). Ramp: color-only → number-only → color+number two-slot commands. This level is the true integration test — analytics watch it as the predicted difficulty spike.

### Level 5 — "The Bridge II: Paint & Twins" (Chunk Assembly) ⭐
Bridge mechanic returns (copula plank now familiar — pure reinforcement) with two new physical mechanics:
- **Paint Bucket (adjective order):** the color tile is a paint bucket. Placed *before* the noun tile, it pours and colors the noun's picture — "a **red** shell" appears painted. Placed after the noun, the bucket has nothing ahead of it to pour on, tips comically, and hops back; the slots then pulse in the correct order. The English order is the only order in which the physics works.
- **Twin Sticker (plural -s):** an "-s" tile shaped like a twin sticker. Snapping it onto a noun duplicates the picture with a pop: "two star**s**" shows two stars. Sentences requiring it: *Three stars. / It's a red shell. / I can see two shells.* If the child builds "two star" and taps GO, only one star sits on the bridge post, the counter reads "2?", the parrot brings the -s sticker: "two starrrr...S!"
Question bridge: assemble *What / color / is / it?* and *How / many / stars?* as whole chunks.

### Level 6 — "Treasure Dive II"
Standard 90-second dive; due items from Islands 0 + 3 + current. New wrinkle: some treasure requires a two-step answer ("How many bananas?" → tap the group, then tap/say the number word) — first compound retrieval.

### Level 7 — BOSS: "The Rainbow Octopus"
The octopus has lost the colors of its eight arms; the child restores them through conversation.

| Turn | Rainbow Octopus | Response options (✔ advances) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Hello! ... hello? HELLO!" (peeks shyly from cave) | ✔ "Hello!" · ✔ "Hi!" | Island 3 review baked into the boss |
| 2 | Holds up a gray arm next to a red coral: "What color is it?" | ✔ "It's red!" · "Red!" (accepted; octopus models the full chunk back: "Yes! IT'S red!") | full-chunk answers earn the arm *and* a sparkle; bare-word answers earn the arm + a friendly full-form model — communication first, form modeled |
| 3 | Repeats for 3 more arms (blue, yellow, purple), phrasings shuffled | as above | arms visibly recolor — progress is the octopus itself |
| 4 | Points at a cloud of shells: "How many shells?" | ✔ "Six!" · ✔ "Six shells!" | counting overlay lets the child tap-count first if unsure (scaffold, not penalty) |
| 5 | Holds up a mystery bag, a star pokes out: "Look! What is it?" | ✔ "It's a yellow star!" — assembled from 3 tiles (It's a / yellow / star) then optionally spoken | the boss version of the Paint-Bucket sentence; tile assembly keeps it achievable, mic invitation on top |
| 6 | All eight arms rainbow-colored, octopus dances: "Thank you! Goodbye!" | ✔ "Goodbye!" · ✔ "Bye!" | shields ceremony, 25 coins + 1 pearl |

Replay variation: arm colors, shell counts, and the mystery object shuffle every run — the language is stable, the answers aren't memorizable.

## 6. Trading Post Stock (this island)
Colored sails (each named on purchase: "the GREEN sail!"), paint pots for recoloring home-island furniture (recolors are free forever after purchase — a color-word sandbox), a shell-counting abacus decoration, star-lantern strings sold in quantities ("How many lanterns?" → "Five, please!" — the shop now *uses this island's chunks*, per the Trading Post rule). Pearl item: the Rainbow Octopus as a home-island visitor who periodically asks "What color is it?" about owned items.

## 7. Analytics & Adjustment Hooks
- adjective-order error rate in Level 5 (paint-bucket misplacements); target: declining within-island; persistent >30% → schedule Bridge II mini-replay
- plural -s omission rate (tiles and, where mic used, spoken /s/ presence — report only, never gate)
- three/tree discrimination accuracy in 3B (below 70% → extra Parrot Ears round injected before boss)
- two-slot command performance in Level 4 (the flagged difficulty spike; high failure → auto-insert a single-slot warmup)
- standard island metrics per the Island 3 spec (§9)
